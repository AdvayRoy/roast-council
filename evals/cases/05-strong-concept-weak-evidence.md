# Case name

Strong concept with weak evidence

## Input

ROAST: Schools lose hours every week coordinating student event permissions across email, spreadsheets, and paper forms. I understand the workflow from working at one school, and six administrators said a shared approval timeline would be useful. The proposed product is a secure workflow with reminders and audit history. No school has agreed to a paid pilot, no buyer or budget owner is confirmed, and the plan is to build integrations before asking for a commitment. The first target is 20 schools.

## Expected profile

Business / Idea with a Product / Feature secondary lens.

## Expected core behavior

Advocate should identify a real workflow pain and founder insight without treating “useful” interviews as payment evidence. Red Team should focus on buyer, procurement, privacy, integrations, and distribution. Reality Check should name the administrator/decision-maker and propose a concierge pilot or paid pilot with a concrete threshold. Judge should choose `FIX FIRST`, not `BUILD` or `KILL`, and specify the exact evidence needed before integrations.

## Fail conditions

- Presents six positive conversations as verified demand.
- Demands a complete product before a pilot.
- Kills the concept solely because evidence is incomplete.
- Gives a vague request for more research without a buyer or threshold.

## Good verdict range

`FIX FIRST`: secure a defined buyer, a paid or commercially specific pilot, and a bounded manual workflow test before meaningful integration work.

## Notes

The concept can be strong while the evidence remains weak; the verdict should distinguish those claims.
