# Case name

Re-ROAST with evidence strong enough to flip the verdict

## Input

Re-ROAST the school event-permissions workflow from the earlier review. Previous verdict: `FIX FIRST` because interviews showed pain but no buyer or payment evidence. New evidence: the operations director at one school signed a 30-day paid pilot at AED 3,000, supplied the current forms, assigned an implementation owner, and agreed to measure approval turnaround and staff hours saved. A second school booked a commercial pilot call after seeing the manual workflow. The first pilot is still small and does not prove a 20-school market.

## Expected profile

Business / Idea with Re-ROAST behavior.

## Expected core behavior

The output must show `PREVIOUS VERDICT`, `NEW EVIDENCE`, `BELIEFS CHANGED`, `BELIEFS UNCHANGED`, `EVIDENCE QUALITY CHANGE`, `NEW VERDICT`, and `NEXT TEST`. It should update the buyer/payment claim from assumption toward fact, preserve uncertainty about repeatability and distribution, and move to `BUILD` with a narrow pilot/MVP scope or explicitly explain why it remains `FIX FIRST`. Repeating the old verdict without belief delta is a failure.

## Fail conditions

- Treats the paid pilot as irrelevant and repeats the old prose.
- Treats one pilot as proof of a 20-school market.
- Jumps to a broad build without measuring the pilot outcome.
- Claims certainty beyond the evidence.

## Good verdict range

`BUILD` with a narrow pilot-backed scope, or a clearly justified `FIX FIRST` focused on pilot delivery; confidence should remain medium.

## Notes

The new evidence is intentionally strong enough to move the central demand belief but not every business belief.
