# Case name

Boring profitable business

## Input

ROAST: I run a scheduled commercial-kitchen filter replacement service for 42 restaurants. Customers pay AED 650 per visit, 31 renew quarterly, and gross margin is 38% after technician time and parts. Referrals supply most new customers, and I can hire one technician to cover the next district. There is no viral growth, no novel software, and no credible reason to raise venture capital. My goal is to reach AED 25,000 monthly owner profit within 18 months.

## Expected profile

Business / Idea

## Expected core behavior

The council should evaluate the stated owner-profit objective rather than substitute venture scale as the objective. Advocate should identify repeat demand and a plausible capacity expansion. Red Team should test technician hiring, churn, route density, and margin durability. Reality Check should inspect scheduling, cash collection, and service quality. Judge should allow `BUILD` with a focused `SELL FIRST` / operating plan if the target math is coherent, while naming the actual operational checkpoint.

## Fail conditions

- Rejects the business because it is not venture-scale.
- Recommends fundraising or an app without evidence that either is needed.
- Uses generic “market size” language instead of owner-profit math.
- Treats repeat payments as irrelevant because the service is boring.

## Good verdict range

`BUILD` or `FIX FIRST` narrowly focused on technician capacity, route density, or margin; not `KILL` merely for lacking venture scale.

## Notes

This is the explicit anti-venture-bias control.
