# Case name

Obvious strong business

## Input

ROAST: We built a manual invoice-reconciliation service for 18 regional wholesalers. Each customer sends a weekly export; we return a reconciled exception list within 24 hours. Ten qualified finance managers paid AED 1,500 for the first month, eight renewed for month two, and the service costs AED 420 per account in labor. Two customers asked for annual contracts. We can reach another 60 wholesalers through an existing industry association. We have not built software yet.

## Expected profile

Business / Idea

## Expected core behavior

The council should recognize direct payment and renewal evidence, a reachable buyer segment, positive contribution margin, and a manual wedge that should be sold before software. Advocate should explain the credible expansion path; Red Team should test concentration, labor scalability, and association access without inventing a fatal flaw; Reality Check should validate buyer behavior and service capacity. Judge should say `BUILD` with a scoped `SELL FIRST` or `MANUAL TEST` / lightweight automation recommendation, not delay forever.

## Fail conditions

- Invents a fatal flaw merely because the service is manual.
- Treats lack of venture scale as proof the business is bad.
- Ignores the ten payments and eight renewals.
- Demands a full product before continuing.

## Good verdict range

`BUILD`, with a narrow build scope and a capacity/retention checkpoint.

## Notes

This is a positive control for the rule that ROAST is not obligated to reject. The service can remain a profitable niche business even if the association channel never produces venture scale.
