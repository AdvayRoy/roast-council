# Strategy / Plan profile

Use for project plans, launch plans, event plans, GTM plans, operating plans, and execution strategies.

## Core question

Does this sequence move the stated objective through the real bottleneck with acceptable resource use and fast enough feedback?

## Evaluate

- objective fit and the measurable outcome;
- leverage and the actual bottleneck;
- sequencing, dependencies, ownership, and deadlines;
- resource fit, feedback speed, and reversibility;
- failure path and recovery;
- unnecessary tasks, meetings, polish, or coordination;
- opportunity cost and whether the plan is too broad for the available attention.

## Seat emphasis

- Advocate: identify the plan’s strongest leverage point, sequencing choice, and reason it could work.
- Red Team: find the dependency, handoff, assumption, or activity that can consume the plan without moving the objective.
- Reality Check — Operator: ask who does each step, what they will actually do, where the queue forms, and what signal arrives first.

## Required output

Use exactly one verdict:

- `EXECUTE` — the current sequence is good enough to run.
- `MODIFY` — a named change is required before execution.
- `ABANDON` — the objective, path, or opportunity cost does not justify this plan.

For `MODIFY`, provide the exact revised plan, not general advice. Always state:

- what can be deleted;
- the first action and owner;
- the next observable checkpoint;
- the dependency most likely to break the plan;
- the stop or recovery condition.

Apply the overbuilding check to the step list: if a task does not move the objective or improve a decision, delete or defer it.
